💊 Supplement corner

Food covers most of this, but these are the categories worth asking your doctor about by name — they're inexpensive and widely available at any local pharmacy. Don't start doses on your own; get them confirmed based on your actual blood levels.

💊 Worth asking about

Vitamin D3 (sachet or tablet)Omega-3 / fish oil, or 2–3 fish meals a week instead Vitamin B12 (already checked fine, so only if it drops)Plain multivitamin, if diet is inconsistent

⛔ Skip on your own

Iron tablets — not without a doctor's confirmation, given carrier status Random "ayurvedic" blood boostersHigh-dose vitamin D without a retest plan

📌 Almanac notes

  • Carrier status: flag thalassemia trait to any new doctor before anyone reflexively suggests iron — your iron studies have been normal each time.
  • Lipids: recheck the full lipid profile after ~3 months of consistent movement and lower sugar, to see if HDL and triglycerides are actually moving.
  • Vitamin D: the goal this time is holding steady, not just correcting once — keep the maintenance routine going even after levels look fine.
  • Slow trends: mention the TSH trend at your next visit, even though it's still in range.
Not a medical plan — pair this with your doctor's guidance.
